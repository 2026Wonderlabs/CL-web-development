About Red Bull Racing

A legacy of innovation and excellence

Oracle Red Bull Racing is a Formula One racing team and constructor, competing under the Austrian flag. The team is headquartered in Milton Keynes, United Kingdom, with a technology centre in Milton Keynes and a factory in Milton Keynes.

Founded in 2005, Red Bull Racing has become one of the most successful teams in Formula 1 history, with six Constructors' Championships and multiple Drivers' Championships. Our success is built on a foundation of innovation, engineering excellence, and an unwavering commitment to pushing the boundaries of motorsport technology.
